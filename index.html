Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. We design, build and set up everything for you.

When do I send my photos?

After we agree on a price. To start, the form just asks who you are, where people can find you, and what the site is for.

How does payment work?

You don’t pay anything until you’ve agreed on the price. We’ll explain how to pay when we send your quote.

Can I use a domain I already own?

Often, yes. Mention it under “Anything else” in the form and we’ll go through it with you during setup.

What if the domain I want is taken?

It depends on what’s available. We’ll help you find an option you like. Premium domains may cost extra.

Can I make changes after launch?

Ongoing edits and any costs for them are confirmed with you before you buy. Ask about them in your request.
