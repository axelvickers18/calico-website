Questions

What do I need to send?

To start, just the form. After we reply with a price, you send your photos, videos, a few lines about yourself, and the links you want on the site.

I’m not good with tech. Is that OK?

Yes. You don’t need to know anything about websites. We design it, build it and set it up. You send your photos and tell us what you like.

I already have my own link. Can I use it?

Usually, yes. Put it in the form and we’ll tell you how it works.

What if the name I want is taken?

We’ll suggest available options, like a variation of your name or a different ending, and you pick one. Premium domains may cost extra.

What happens to my current link in bio page?

Nothing. Keep it as long as you like. When your site is ready, you swap the link in your bio for your new one.

How can I request changes after launch?

Email us with what you’d like changed. Before you buy, we’ll confirm what’s included after launch and any ongoing costs.
